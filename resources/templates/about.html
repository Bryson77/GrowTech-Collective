Our Story

Built to Grow Communities

GrowTech Collective is where technology meets the soil — bridging the gap between modern innovation and the growers who need it most.

Mission

Why We Exist

GrowTech Collective was founded by Lethabo Mabilo, a visionary who sought to use technological skills to help end food insecurity in communities — and ultimately the world. We believe that innovation and collaboration can transform the way communities grow and share food.

Our vision is to combine modern technology with sustainable practices to empower people, reduce hunger, and create opportunities for growth. Through teamwork, creativity, and dedication, we aim to inspire change and make a lasting impact on society and the environment.

Platform

What We Offer

Our platform brings together research, practical guides, and interactive tools to help communities, schools, and households start and maintain their own food gardens. It includes detailed case studies, planting guides, soil and fertilizer advice tailored to South African conditions, and simple calculators to plan garden layouts efficiently.
